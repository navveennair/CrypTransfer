<objective>
Fix the receiver stepper 3 prefill ordering bug: _onSenderSelected is currently called in initState before the mailbox is open, causing selectSenderHandshake to throw KeyNotFoundException because the public key has not yet been written to Firestore. The fix defers the pre-filled partner selection until _onToggleReady(true) completes, after setupReceiverSession has published the public key.

Purpose: This is the root cause of UAT test 10 failure ("stepper 3 does not recognize stepper 0"). Fixing it unblocks end-to-end transfer via the neutral handshake flow.
Output: receiver_screen.dart with _pendingPrefilledSender field and deferred consumption in _onToggleReady(true).
</objective>
